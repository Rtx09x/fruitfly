What is—and is not—modeled

All available neurons and edges are loaded. Synapse count sets edge magnitude; transmitter labels set an assumed sign. The model omits morphology, delays, gap junctions, receptor types, plasticity, muscles and biomechanics. Do not infer emotions, intent, or circuit function from activity.
